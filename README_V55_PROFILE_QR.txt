FLEVO v5.5 — Dynamic Profile QR

1) profile.html is the permanent QR destination.
2) Open settings.html and use “رفع وتحديث البروفايل”.
3) The PDF is uploaded with a fixed Storage path and company_settings.profile_pdf_url is updated.
4) The QR never needs reprinting because it points to profile.html, not directly to the PDF.
5) After deployment, open profile.html and click “تحميل QR للطباعة” to download a PNG QR containing the final production domain.
6) For local Live Server testing, QR contains localhost and is not intended for printing.
